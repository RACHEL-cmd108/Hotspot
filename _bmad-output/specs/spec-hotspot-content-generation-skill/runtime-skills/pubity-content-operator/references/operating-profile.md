# Operating Profile

## Observed baseline

A public 100-post sample captured 2026-08-04 estimated 17.77 posts/day, 100% original packaging, and up to three posts in 60 minutes. Strong sampled themes included birthdays, national progress, renewable energy, science, box-office milestones, and cultural scale comparisons.

## Hook families

- `Today is [person]'s [age] birthday.`
- `[Metric] has grown X-fold since [year].`
- `[Work/project] achieved X before [milestone].`
- `[Country/community] now gets X% of [system] from [source].`
- `[Person/researcher] discovered [surprising fact].`
- `You would need X hours/days to [complete cultural task].`

## Fact-check branches

- Energy: distinguish electricity from total energy, demand from generation, temporary intervals from continuous supply, and imports/exports/storage.
- Science: distinguish demonstration, correlation, preliminary result, and consensus.
- National statistics: state denominator, year, nominal/real terms, and source.
- Human-interest: protect privacy and avoid exploiting children, animals, or tragedy.

## Comment routing

- Authoritative correction: verify, acknowledge, update.
- Missing scope/denominator: clarify and revise if material.
- Cultural context: add when it prevents misunderstanding.
- Generic reaction: no reply required.

Final test: is the fact specific, verified, understandable, and shareable without misleading omission?
