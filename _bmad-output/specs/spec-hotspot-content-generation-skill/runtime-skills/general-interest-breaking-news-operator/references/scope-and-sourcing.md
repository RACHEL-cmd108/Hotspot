# General-interest desk scope and sourcing

## Publishable source order

1. Direct institutional announcement, official document, event organizer, league, school, city, company, or public authority.
2. Attributable named wire or credible publication.
3. A verified direct statement from the responsible person or organization.

Treat unattributed screenshots, viral clips without provenance, and repost chains as leads only.

## Scope check

| Event shape | Route |
|---|---|
| Consumer, platform, education, city, cultural institution, ordinary sport, or non-sensitive public development | This Skill |
| AI / technology | AI / Tech fast desk |
| Finance, crypto, policy institution | WatcherGuru desk |
| Money, corporate power, regulation, consumer costs | Unusual Whales desk |
| Celebrity, entertainment, creator culture | Daily Loud desk |
| Death, harm, disaster, public safety, crime, health emergency, private tragedy | Skip; do not force a general-interest alert |
