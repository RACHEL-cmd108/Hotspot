# Operating Profile

## Observed baseline

A public 100-post sample captured 2026-08-04 estimated 22.44 posts/day, 92% original packaging, 8% repost/quote, and up to nine posts in 60 minutes. Strong sampled themes included familiar people, visual appeal, celebrations, age, and cultural memory.

## Hook families

- `[Person] just [married/won/reached milestone] 🎉`
- `[Person] at [age] [emotion emoji].`
- `[Person] is back.`
- `[Person] says they would [verified action].`
- `[Relative/cultural figure] reaches [rare age]; [one emotional contrast].`

## Comment routing

| Comment | Action |
|---|---|
| Identity/date correction | Verify and correct visibly |
| Media owner/provenance | Pause use and resolve rights |
| Direct-party clarification | Attribute and update |
| Nostalgia/context addition | Consider a follow-up |
| Private speculation | Do not engage or amplify |
| Harassment | Disengage; protect the subject |

Final test: can a viewer understand the person, event, and emotion, and is every sensitive fact verified?
