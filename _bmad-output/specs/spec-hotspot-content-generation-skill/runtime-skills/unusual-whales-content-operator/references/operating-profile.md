# Operating Profile

## Observed baseline

A public 100-post sample captured 2026-08-04 estimated 24.91 posts/day, 94% original packaging, 6% repost/quote, and up to six posts in 60 minutes.

## Topic scan

- Lawsuits and allegations involving institutions.
- Executive compensation versus stakeholder outcomes.
- Profit, wealth, prices, and consumer cost.
- Regulation, lobbying, public money, and political finance.
- Market-moving filings, trades, and policy changes.

## Hook families

- `[Institution] is being sued; the complaint alleges [conduct].`
- `[Executive] received $X while [separately verified stakeholder outcome].`
- `[Profit/wealth] rose X% while [cost burden] reached Y.`
- `[Named source] estimates [actor] made $X from [mechanism].`
- `JUST IN:` for verified time-sensitive changes.

## Reply rules

- Source document: verify and surface.
- Denominator/time-period correction: acknowledge and amend.
- Direct-party statement: attribute; do not treat as dispositive automatically.
- Interpretation dispute: answer only when it exposes a factual premise.
- Material correction: standalone update, not a buried reply.

Keep commercial promotion visually and verbally distinct from editorial content.

Final test: who gained or paid, how much, by what mechanism, and according to which source?
