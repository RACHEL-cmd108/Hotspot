# Operating Cadence and Interaction

## Cadence Model

Use an event-driven rhythm:

`quiet monitoring → trigger → quick signal → investigation → analysis → selective debate → resolution/postmortem → quiet monitoring`

Do not translate this into a mandatory daily posting calendar. A useful weekly system may contain many observations and zero posts.

## Watchlist Design

Track:

- markets with material liquidity or governance consequences;
- unresolved markets with ambiguous terms;
- known platform rule changes;
- anomalous volume, new-wallet concentration, and price gaps;
- official sources likely to determine resolution;
- claims made in prior posts that are nearing a falsification point.

For each item, store the price, timestamp, rule link, resolution source, thesis, trigger threshold, and position disclosure.

## Trigger Levels

- `Observe`: weak anomaly, no verified edge.
- `Signal`: one verified time-sensitive fact.
- `Explain`: a mechanism makes the fact useful.
- `Investigate`: several sources or actors must be reconciled.
- `Audit`: the outcome permits a postmortem.

Move down as well as up. New evidence can kill a draft.

## Interaction Logic

Treat replies as an evidence market.

Prioritize:

1. primary evidence that changes the timeline;
2. a counterexample that changes probability;
3. clarification from an involved institution or expert;
4. questions exposing a recurring misunderstanding;
5. humor that strengthens community identity without targeting a person.

Ignore or stop engaging with:

- repeated claims already answered twice;
- demands for certainty where the evidence is probabilistic;
- identity speculation, doxxing, or coordinated pile-ons;
- engagement bait with no factual or mechanism-level content.

## High-Stakes Reply Template

1. Restate the strongest version of the objection.
2. Identify the decisive evidence.
3. Explain the relevant rule or incentive.
4. State the current probability or position, not absolute certainty.
5. Name what would reverse the conclusion.

## Low-Stakes Reply Template

Use one factual correction or one original joke. Stop before the joke becomes the argument.

## Review Metrics

Do not optimize primarily for post count or raw impressions. Review:

- percentage of posts with a verifiable information increment;
- speed from verified trigger to useful signal;
- percentage of claims with disclosed update conditions;
- correction rate and time to correction;
- saves, bookmarks, and high-signal replies;
- follow-up accuracy after resolution;
- ratio of researched observations to published posts;
- whether long posts were actually necessary.

## Safety and Credibility

- Disclose holdings and material platform relationships.
- Avoid language that could be read as coordinated pumping.
- Do not infer trader identity from wallet behavior.
- Separate unusual timing from proof of leaked information.
- Preserve private individuals' privacy.
- Use primary sources for sensitive political, regulatory, or settlement claims.
- Correct errors visibly and update the thesis when falsifiers occur.
