# Operating Profile

## Observed baseline

A public 100-post sample captured 2026-08-04 estimated 13.76 posts/day, 97% original packaging, 3% repost/quote, and up to four posts in 60 minutes.

## Priority stack

1. Official policy, regulatory, or institutional action.
2. Large market moves or monetary amounts.
3. Consequential statements by decision-makers.
4. Security, theft, or infrastructure events.
5. Live proceedings that update expectations.

## Templates

- `JUST IN: [anchor] [subject] [action].`
- `BREAKING: [high-consequence confirmed event].`
- `LIVE: [person/institution] [event].`
- `$X billion/trillion [added, lost, bought, stolen].`
- `[Authority] calls on [institution] to [action].`

## Comment and correction policy

- New primary evidence: verify and update.
- Minor wording correction: acknowledge briefly.
- Core fact changes: standalone correction with the original claim and new source.
- Interpretation dispute: no reply unless a factual premise is wrong.
- Rumor amplification: no public monitoring post.

Final test: can a reader identify who did what, when, and according to whom in one scan?
